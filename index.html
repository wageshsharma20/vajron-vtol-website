  • Safe stand-off operation without flying unnecessarily close to targets

Automated mission flow

  1. Pre-flight verificationSystem health, calibration status, battery condition and environmental suitability checked before every flight.
  2. Safe take-offTake-off is denied from incorrect or unsafe locations.
  3. Cruise navigationTerrain-aware navigation and smart path planning on repeatable, precise flight paths.
  4. Obstacle-aware flightDual straight-beam LiDAR and dual fixed cameras watch the flight path.
  5. Live intelligence captureDetections are geo-tagged and streamed to the ground control station.
  6. Autonomous returnSmart return-to-launch and an obstacle-aware vertical landing.

Edge AI on board

Terrain-aware planning

Secure communications

Built for contested electromagnetic environments

An anti-jam link, an optional jam-immune tether and end-to-end encryption.

Frequency hopping

A frequency-hopping spread-spectrum link with rapid, continuous channel-hopping to defeat narrowband and swept-frequency jamming.

Optional fibre-optic tether

A tethered fibre-optic data-link mode for fully jam-immune operation in heavily contested environments.

End-to-end encryption

Encrypted command, control and video data links, and a highly encrypted ground-station link.

Link resilience

  • Resilient link architecture designed to maintain mission continuity under electronic-warfare conditions
  • Rapid, continuous channel-hopping against narrowband and swept-frequency jamming
  • Optional fibre-optic tethered mode for jam-immune operation
  • Automatic fail-safe: Smart RTL if link integrity cannot be maintained

Ground control station

  • Dedicated, secure GCS with live telemetry and a mission-planning interface
  • Live video and AI detection-event feed displayed in real time
  • Mission logging, playback and post-mission analysis
  • Emergency manual RTL and override controls
  • Encrypted data storage and access-controlled mission records
